Approved direction: a short story told through real app evidence, edited in
Remotion, with Facundo recording the final voiceover. Target: 2:45, including
the end card. The editable project is in [video/](../video/README.md), with a
separate [voiceover recording sheet](./demo-voiceover.md).

First-cut phase completed September 6, 2026: silent and rehearsal MP4s are in
`video/out/buy-hard-silent.mp4` and `video/out/buy-hard-cues.mp4`. Both contain
4,950 frames at 30 fps, 1920×1080: 2:45 of picture. Type checking, capture
coverage, continuous scene timing, and exported-frame visual checks passed.
The exports are local and ignored by Git; the committed source and PNGs recreate
them. Final narration, optional deli footage, and submission publishing remain.

The first cut uses genuine browser screenshots with editorial camera moves.
The attempted browser-motion capture cropped the right edge, so it is excluded
from the film. Source screenshots remain intact; no UI or emails are recreated.
The owner scene uses the real Buy Desk overview until deli footage is supplied.

## The story

“Why would I pay four hundred dollars more for deli lids?”

Open on the actual comparison: a $2,600 quote beside the $3,000 recommendation.
Let the viewer wonder. Cut briefly to Facundo's existing deli footage, then
follow the purchase backward to its shortage and forward through its evidence.
Missing supplier information becomes the complication. The reveal is that the
cheaper offer arrives after the required date, with six projected stockout days.
The human decision makes sense once the app has assembled the facts.

The ending returns to the deli and the idea of keeping the line moving. Show
confirmed incoming stock as the product outcome, without implying that a shipment
physically arrived at the deli or that the app already operates that business.

The storytelling devices are a question in the opening, a personal reason to care,
an obstacle, a delayed answer, and a return to the opening image with new meaning.
The viewer follows a decision rather than a catalog of features.

## Draft narration for Facundo

Read conversationally, like showing another owner why a decision made sense.
The text below is a recording draft. Scene timings remain adjustable until
the final voice track is fitted.

**0:00 — The question**

Why would I pay four hundred dollars more for deli lids? This is the cheaper
quote. This is the one BUY HARD recommended. The difference matters when you
have customers waiting.

**0:12 — The owner**

I've run a deli. The food gets your attention. So do the customers. But ordinary
things, like having enough containers and lids, have to keep working too.

**0:27 — The shortage**

Here's a recorded demo purchase. The starting stock was three thousand, two
hundred forty lids, with average use of six hundred twelve a day. BUY HARD
calculated the shortage and the deadline. Convex keeps the record and screens
in sync.

**0:46 — The search**

Firecrawl found real supplier pages. OpenAI helped assess the evidence and draft
requests for quotes. AgentMail sent the emails. For this demonstration, supplier
replies came through controlled test inboxes.

**1:06 — The complication**

One quote was incomplete. Freight and arrival were missing. Here is the
clarification the agent actually sent. The next reply still left information
unresolved, so it followed up again. By revision three, the requested terms were
present. You can inspect that whole exchange in the app.

**1:32 — The answer**

Now the cheaper quote makes sense. It arrives after the deadline: six projected
days without stock. SupplyCo costs four hundred dollars more, but arrives in
time. Stored purchase rules make that comparison. The trade-off is visible.

**1:55 — The decision**

Then it's my decision. This is the approval I recorded against the exact purchase
terms. From here, I can inspect the purchase order and the quote revision behind it.

**2:14 — The result**

AgentMail delivered the order once. OpenAI extracted the supplier's confirmation.
The terms matched, and fifteen thousand incoming units were confirmed. The
evidence stays attached to the purchase.

**2:33 — Back to work**

That's why I built BUY HARD. Less time chasing a purchase. More attention for
the business in front of you. Keep the line moving.

## How to produce it

Use **Remotion** as the editing and rendering tool. Its React/TypeScript model
fits this repository and supports precise placement of video, titles and audio.
HyperFrames is a valid HTML/CSS/GSAP alternative, but this film primarily needs
to edit captured footage. The actual application remains the source of every
product frame.

1. Verify the chosen judge host and release. Walk through the selected record
   again; make the narration agree with its current evidence. Reuse the current
   app walkthrough documented in [demo-screenplay.md](./demo-screenplay.md).
2. Capture real app actions at a fixed 1920×1080 viewport, ideally with 2× pixel
   density for modest crops. Keep a normal browser view before each close-up.
   Capture full motion through clicks and transitions, with stills as deliberate
   reading holds. Rendering the film must not itself run live queries or sends.
3. Build a separate `video/` composition project. Keep source media and a scene
   manifest with clip paths, in/out points, crop positions, narration cues and
   durations. Use the app's existing typography and palette for chapter cards.
4. Export a silent 2:45 review cut plus a cue copy with a timecode and the draft
   lines. Test the hook and one complete comparison scene before producing the
   whole film. Check text readability at ordinary playback size.
5. Facundo records the narration after watching that cut. Record each of the nine
   blocks separately, with a short quiet lead-in and tail, or provide one clean
   take. A quiet-room phone recording is enough to begin; WAV is preferred.
6. Fit the scene holds and cut points to his natural delivery. The final timing
   follows his voice; shorten copy or pictures if necessary. Add restrained
   receipt/keyboard sounds and optional quiet music underneath, then create
   captions from the final spoken take.
7. Export a clean 1080p MP4 under three minutes, an accessible caption file, and
   the editable composition. Review the film against the same judge URL before
   adding the video link to the submission materials.

Keep editorial captions outside the captured interface. Do not synthesize
buttons, emails, state transitions or numbers for the video. “Recorded run”
must stay clear when cuts compress supplier waiting time. The hook describes
a trade-off, not measured savings, actual lost sales or a physically received
shipment. Preserve demo-assumption and source labels when relevant to a claim.
