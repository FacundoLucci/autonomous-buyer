# BUY HARD demo walkthrough

Target runtime: under three minutes. Use the app's recorded-run guide, not a fresh
purchase. The guide only navigates; it does not sign in, send email, or approve anything.

## Setup

- Open the [development review app](https://festive-coyote-483.convex.site/).
- Stay signed out. Click **Demo** to select the latest confirmed
  purchase in the current scenario.
- The production URL still hosts an older release. Do not use it to review this design.
- Confirm the selected record, dates, quantities and provider credits before recording.
  These come from stored evidence and may differ after an authorized scenario reset.
- Keep credentials, personal tabs and notifications out of the recording.

## Six stops

| Time      | Guide stop                              | What to inspect                                                                                                                                                                        |
| --------- | --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0:00–0:20 | Start with the shortage                 | Required quantity, deadline and original usage calculation. Convex powers live updates.                                                                                                |
| 0:20–0:45 | Inspect the supplier sources            | Open a real source link. Firecrawl found the pages; completed model work carries its actual provider credit. Website discoveries are distinct from controlled supplier inboxes.        |
| 0:45–1:20 | Watch an incomplete quote become usable | Trace a missing field to **Read sent email**, then the next linked supplier revision. A sent clarification is credited to AgentMail. Incomplete or conflicting replies remain labeled. |
| 1:20–1:50 | Compare the actual trade-offs           | Latest supplier offers side by side, including cost, arrival, purchase rules and projected stockout days. The summary is derived from stored numbers, not generated prose.             |
| 1:50–2:15 | The buyer keeps control                 | The recorded decision and exact purchase terms. Do not make a new approval. **Inspect PO** continues to the confirmation step.                                                         |
| 2:15–2:45 | Close the loop with supplier evidence   | Supplier-confirmed quantity and arrival, matching terms and the purchase order document. AgentMail and the actual extraction provider are credited beside their work.                  |

Use **Next** at each stop. **Finish** or closing the guide restores the view used
before starting the walkthrough. A directly opened tour link returns to the Open buys
queue. The everyday app exposes the same quote history, comparison and order evidence
without a demo narrator.

## Everyday entry

- **Open buys** is the default queue, using the same server-owned open flag as the
  metric. Reviews and exceptions come first, then required date.
- **Recent buys** keeps the latest linked purchase per inventory item accessible,
  including confirmed purchases. **Inventory** remains a separate tab.
- The **Demo** button selects recorded evidence directly; an empty Open buys queue
  does not prevent the walkthrough. It never reopens a completed purchase.
- The **BUY HARD** home link resets the list and filters to Open buys. Returning from
  an individual purchase keeps the list view and filters used to open it.

## Claims to keep precise

- Real provider requests and deliveries can coexist with controlled test recipients.
  Do not imply that discovered businesses sent the controlled inbox replies.
- Product-match assumptions in controlled quotes are not measured confidence.
- Annual spend and savings labeled **Demo history** are seeded history, not savings
  demonstrated by this purchase.
- **Configured** means credentials are present, not that a live health check passed.
- A supplier confirmation is not a physical goods receipt.
- State a supplier's price or arrival only as it appears in the selected record.
