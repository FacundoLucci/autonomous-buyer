Primary outcomes: relevant inbound messages, hiring conversations, interviews, useful founder introductions, and YC-related feedback or application progress. Track each actual stage separately. The final outcomes are an offer or acceptance; attention alone does not establish either.

Supporting signals: visits to the profile or demo when measurable, saves, meaningful replies and sponsor amplification. Keep operator inquiries and pilot interest in the product campaign's scorecard. A builder reply is relevant to this campaign even if it does not become a customer.
