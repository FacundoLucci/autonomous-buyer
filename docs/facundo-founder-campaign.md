# Facundo — founder and builder campaign

September 12, 2026 · Separate from the [BUY HARD product campaign](gtm-marketing-strategy.md)

**Internal goal:** Turn hackathon attention into a strong job opportunity or progress toward a place at Y Combinator. BUY HARD is the work people can inspect; Facundo is the person this campaign introduces. YC is an internal aim, not a public hook or named audience in the posts.

The product campaign continues to sell staying stocked, delegated buying and walkthroughs. This campaign shows the thinking behind the product: choosing a useful problem, simplifying an overwhelming frontend, connecting agent tools, and improving the result. Each campaign has its own copy, tracking and outcomes.

## Positioning

**I build AI products that take useful work off people's plates.**

Show a concrete decision and its result. Make the design polished and the writing memorable. Use ambitious questions for the future vision. Describe personal experience and results accurately; do not invent customers, revenue, team history, job titles or YC affiliation.

## Personal voice and visuals

Facundo is relaxed, positive and open. Write like someone sharing work he enjoys and inviting people to talk. Use natural first-person language, contractions and specific observations. Keep the invitations easy: “Curious what you think,” “I’d love to compare notes,” or “Say hi.” Avoid a formal founder pitch, self-congratulatory claims, forced motivational lines and explicit YC appeals. A friendly invitation to discuss work can still support the hiring goal.

Give each post supporting images that show what the caption refers to. ## Turn attention into opportunities

The immediate route is a reply or message to Facundo on the account that published the post. Include one clear invitation in the dedicated opportunity posts. Use the product demo as the work sample. Send interested operators to the product's existing walkthrough path; a hiring conversation should not be presented as a purchasing pilot.

Prepare a compact founder portfolio around the best work: a one-sentence introduction, the current demo, one design decision, one agent workflow, and a short account of what changed during the hackathon. Save useful public post links as they go live. Keep the founder introduction and product demo as separate assets for a future application. YC's own [application advice](https://www.ycombinator.com/howtoapply) emphasizes clear explanations and specific accomplishments; its [founder-video instructions](https://www.ycombinator.com/video) distinguish the introduction from the product demo.

No claim that Facundo has applied to YC or been accepted is part of this campaign. The intended outcome is progress toward applying successfully: relevant conversations, informed feedback, and a compelling body of work.

## Measure and adjust

Primary outcomes: relevant inbound messages, hiring conversations, interviews, useful founder introductions, and YC-related feedback or application progress. Track each actual stage separately. The final outcomes are an offer or acceptance; attention alone does not establish either.

Supporting signals: visits to the profile or demo when measurable, saves, meaningful replies and sponsor amplification. Keep operator inquiries and pilot interest in the product campaign's scorecard. A builder reply is relevant to this campaign even if it does not become a customer.

At the existing 07:30 and 15:30 reviews, look at both campaigns separately. Record the useful response, the next piece of evidence to show, and any product improvement it suggests. Let a good founder conversation influence the next founder post while keeping the product story coherent. Record missing metrics as unavailable.
