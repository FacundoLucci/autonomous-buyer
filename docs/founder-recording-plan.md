## A recording setup that is enough

- Prop the iPhone at eye level, about an arm’s length away. Use ordinary Video mode, vertically, at 1080p/30 fps; existing 4K recordings are also usable. Keep your head and shoulders centered with a little space around them.
- Face a window or a lamp. Put the light in front of you, slightly to one side. A plain part of the basement is fine; a curtain, rug or soft furnishings nearby can help with the room sound.
- Look at the lens for the opening and closing. Read the middle sentence by sentence if that is easier; the product visuals can cover those cuts.
- Leave two seconds before and after. If you stumble, pause and repeat just that sentence. One or two takes is plenty. Keep your usual speaking voice.
- Send the original MOV or MP4 here, labeled **F01**. No music, captions, filters or trims needed. AirDrop to the Mac first if convenient. We can also work from a local file path you provide.

Codex handles selecting takes, trimming pauses, matching visuals, sound cleanup, captions, exports and the scheduled-post replacement. If one sentence is unusable, request that sentence only. An audio-only middle section is usable when the camera take feels difficult.

## How the edits will work

Use **your actual recording as the voice and camera source**. The default short is 25–40 seconds: roughly 3–5 seconds of you opening, the middle over images or product footage with an occasional small camera frame, then 3–5 seconds of you closing. The footage determines the timing. Readable product details matter more than fitting every source image on screen at once.

Hyperframes assembles the new shorts: real camera clips, existing images, product captures, chapter labels and captions. Use BUY HARD’s lime, pale green and dark colors. Captions show a short phrase at a time, at most two lines, with restrained emphasis on a few words. Correct “BUY HARD,” names, prices and numbers by listening; captions follow the actual spoken take, not this draft. Keep them above platform controls and away from the product decision. Deliver a captioned MP4, a clean MP4, SRT subtitles and a cover image. Start with a square 1080×1080 feed version for X/LinkedIn; make a 1080×1920 version when the layout remains readable.
