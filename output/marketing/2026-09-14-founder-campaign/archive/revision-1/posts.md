@convex @OpenAI @firecrawl @agentmail

**LinkedIn**

Give an agent a job.

For BUY HARD, that job is recurring purchasing: understand stock, research supplies, prepare the next buy, and bring the spending decision to the owner.

The tools need to connect around it. Convex holds the shared state and scheduled work. OpenAI helps research and prepare the buy. Firecrawl reads product pages. AgentMail gives the buyer an inbox.

The part I find interesting is deciding what the agent can carry forward and when a person needs to decide.

Founders building agents: where do you draw that handoff? I'd like to compare approaches.
