That's the kind of update your buyer should bring to you.

BUY HARD uses your stock counts, usage, buying rules, and incoming deliveries to work out when a recurring item needs replenishing. With replenishment enabled, it starts preparing the buy without waiting for you to open a chat and ask.

OpenAI helps the buyer research options and prepare the purchase. You review the terms and approve spending.
