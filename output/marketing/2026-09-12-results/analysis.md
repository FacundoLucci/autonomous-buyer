1. Answer the public question with a concrete explanation and a short app demonstration of a reorder being prepared in the background. This can support both the product benefit and your skill as a builder. No reply was sent during this review.
2. Test another everyday-supply question paired with actual app footage. Give LinkedIn priority for conversation follow-up while retaining X for the personal campaign. Use one clear invitation: bring one recurring item to a walkthrough. Sunday's 12:30 p.m. post already makes that invitation.
3. Resolve the known demo-tracking gap before judging demo engagement. The tested fix is recorded as local commit `59277a1`, not released. This review did not establish a later production deployment. The gap also affected the walkthrough suggestion after a sample approval. [Fix and verification record](../2026-09-12-daily-campaign/reviews/2026-09-12-demo-engagement-fix.md).
4. Keep the approved cadence through the next comparable review window. Compare posts after 24 and 48 hours, separately for the product and personal campaigns. Track substantive replies, operator conversations, bookings and relevant professional introductions alongside visits. There is not enough evidence yet to increase volume or declare a format successful.

**Measurement limits**

A recorded visitor is a browser storage ID, not a verified person. Own visits and bots may be included. Visit events are deduplicated by visitor and event across the stored history, so returning visitors following a different post can be missed. No reliable click-through rate can be calculated from these records and social impressions. Zero recorded demo use is inconclusive because of the known tracking gap. Missing social metrics mean unavailable, not zero. Follower growth and sponsor amplification have not been established.

This review saved evidence and recommendations; it did not change content, schedules or production.
